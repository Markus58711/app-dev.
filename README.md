# My Favorite Series: Stranger Things

## Introduction

My favorite series is **Stranger Things**, a science-fiction and horror television series created by the Duffer Brothers.

### About the Series

The story takes place in the fictional town of Hawkins, Indiana, where a group of friends encounters supernatural events and mysterious creatures from another dimension called the *Upside Down*.

## Main Characters

- **Eleven** – A girl with extraordinary supernatural abilities.
- **Mike Wheeler** – One of Eleven's closest friends.
- **Dustin Henderson** – An intelligent and funny member of the group.
- **Lucas Sinclair** – A brave and loyal friend.
- **Will Byers** – The boy whose disappearance starts the story.

## Why I Like This Series

1. It has an interesting storyline.
2. The characters have strong friendships.
3. It combines mystery, science fiction, and horror.
4. Every season introduces exciting discoveries.
